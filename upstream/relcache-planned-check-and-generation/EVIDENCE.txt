     in every statement.

     back in every statement.
   - Index expressions are parsed and simplified once per relcache entry (RelationGetIndexExpressions(), relcache.c line
     5140) and domain CHECK constraints planned once per backend (load_domaintype_info(), typcache.c line 1221).
