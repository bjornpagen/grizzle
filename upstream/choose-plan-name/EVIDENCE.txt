6. THE CAUSE, IN THE SOURCE (master 4931dab89d, src/backend/optimizer/plan/planner.c)
   - choose_plan_name(), line 9283: without always_number, the name is searched for in glob->subplanNames with
     foreach_ptr/strcmp (9296); if found, or with always_number, "for (n = 1; true; ++n)" (9320) psprintf()s
     "%s_%u" and scans the whole list again (9325) for each n.
   - Callers: subselect.c make_subplan() (sublink kinds "exists", "array", "expr", "any", ... with always_number,
     and "exists_to_any"), SS_process_ctes() (CTE names), allpaths.c set_subquery_pathlist() (FROM-clause aliases,
     "unnamed_subquery" for those without one), prepunion.c ("setop", always numbered), planagg.c ("minmax").
   - glob->subplanNames (pathnodes.h line 187) is read by nothing else in core or contrib.
