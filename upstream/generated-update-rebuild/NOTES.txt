grizzle's side (lane FILLMEM): every UPDATE grizzle emits, a backfill's, a fill's, a write's ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE and
MERGE UPDATE among them, gets its SET list from one door, packages/engine/src/sql/generation.ts assignments(), which

list feeds none (the workaround: then ExecInitGenerated needs at least one expression and keeps its array). That
module says when to delete it: once production runs a release whose ExecInitGenerated keeps the array, or a flag that
nothing is needed. v3 (README.txt) is the first kind; no release has it yet, and none will until upstream commits a
fix and ships it in an 18 or 19 minor release.
